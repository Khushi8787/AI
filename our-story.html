GLAMORA is a premium beauty and jewellery marketplace that brings together buying, selling, renting and safety verification in one platform. We believe every product deserves a longer, more beautiful life.

HOW BUYING WORKS

  1. Browse curated beauty & jewellery
  2. Select your product
  3. Add to cart
  4. Checkout securely
  5. Receive delivery

HOW SELLING WORKS

  1. Submit your eligible product
  2. Safety Verification begins
  3. Get approval (or feedback)
  4. Product is listed
  5. Complete the sale

HOW RENTING WORKS

  1. Select a rental product
  2. Choose start & end dates
  3. Check availability
  4. Book & pay deposit
  5. Enjoy & return on time

HOW SAFETY VERIFICATION WORKS

  1. Product is submitted
  2. Information check
  3. Condition check
  4. Expiry check (cosmetics)
  5. Admin review & approval
GLAMORA VERIFIED ✓

Our verification system helps ensure that eligible resale and rental listings meet basic product, condition and information requirements before being listed on the marketplace.

Important

Expired cosmetics cannot be approved for resale. Rental focuses on reusable jewellery, beauty tools and event essentials — not personal-use cosmetics.
